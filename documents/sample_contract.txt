MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and between ACME Corporation ("Client") and TechServices LLC ("Provider").

1. PAYMENT TERMS
Client agrees to pay all undisputed invoices within Net 90 days from the date of invoice receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

2. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing at least 15 days prior written notice to the other party.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information disclosed during the term of this Agreement and for a period of 5 years following termination.

4. DATA PROTECTION
Provider agrees to implement industry-standard encryption for all Client data in transit and at rest. In the event of a security breach, Provider shall notify Client within 48 hours of discovery.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed $100,000. Indirect, consequential, and punitive damages are expressly excluded.
