VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement is dated February 15, 2026, between Beta Holdings ("Client") and Digital Ops Inc ("Provider").

1. PAYMENT
All undisputed invoices shall be paid within Net 90 days from the invoice date. Late fees of 2% per month apply.

2. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 20 days advance written notice.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
The parties agree to maintain mutual confidentiality of disclosed trade secrets for 5 years after expiration of the term.

4. DATA PRIVACY
Provider shall maintain data encryption standards and shall inform Client within 48 hours of any data security breach.

5. LIABILITY
The total aggregate liability of either party shall not exceed $50,000. Indirect and consequential damages are waived.
