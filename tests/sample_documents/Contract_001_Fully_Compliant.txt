SOFTWARE LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on March 1, 2026, by and between Alpha Global Corp ("Customer") and CloudServices International ("Vendor").

1. PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within Net 30 days of receipt.

2. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing at least 30 days prior written notice.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties mutually agree to protect proprietary and confidential information disclosed under this Agreement for a period of 4 years following any termination.

4. DATA PROTECTION & SECURITY
Vendor shall encrypt all Customer data at rest and in transit using AES-256 standards. In the event of a suspected or confirmed data security incident, Vendor must notify Customer within 24 hours.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Each party's maximum aggregate liability shall be capped at 1.0x the total fees paid under this Agreement in the preceding 12 months. Consequential, special, and indirect damages are expressly excluded.
